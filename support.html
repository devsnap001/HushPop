Remove Ads didn't come back on a new phone

Open Settings (⚙︎) › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

I hear no sound

Check Settings › Sound in the app and your volume. Hush Pop plays even in silent mode.

Turn off statistics or change ad choices

Settings › Share Usage Statistics. Where required, Settings › Ad Privacy Choices. Tracking: iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
